The Chief of Staff's conversation follows the v56 design more closely:
- It opens with the Chief's 64 px hello instead of a small header pill, and each reply sits beside its 24 px pixelkin instead of under a name.
- While a reply is on its way, the waiting line moves through short progress steps (for example "Thinking it through…"), shows steps the AI Client reports such as "Checking memory…", and gives way as soon as the reply starts arriving.
- The "Stop Conversation" control is no longer offered on the Chief of Staff's home conversation.

A Chief of Staff conversation without a recorded reasoning effort now switches to a low one with your next message, using the normal settings change, so replies start sooner.
