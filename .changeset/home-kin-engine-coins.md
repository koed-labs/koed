Home's Conversation coins are now drawn by kin-engine, with the same renderer as the Kin. Coins render at the display's density (or as crisp pixel art with the classic Kin resolution), carry their AI Client's mark, gleam when active today and tarnish after 30 days. A hovered coin stands up to face you. Without WebGL2, Home keeps the vector coin.
