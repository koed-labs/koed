In Solo, Now is your conversation with your Chief of Staff: one persistent chat, shown with their name and look, that continues where you left off. It lives in a Chief of Staff Project kept out of the Project lists.

The Desktop now finds the local API from the running server's own record (and a quick readiness check) instead of running `koed status`, which took 13–37 seconds on a busy Mac. Agents, including the Chief of Staff, load again on their own if Koed was not ready yet.
