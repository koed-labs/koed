# Project Share Policy backend lifecycle

Project Share Policy is a per-member, per-Project authority for new **Bundled** summaries of the owner's exact Captured Sessions. It never grants Team access to Personal source content. An already activated continuous grant retains its own consent and can advance to later sanitized `lcm_leaves` revisions after the policy is revoked. Curated Memory is a separate disclosure and Step-up decision for the entire eligible direct source set.

The owner-local edge constructs an initial manifest from current Personal source and Project mapping revisions. It includes bounded redacted labels, exact Session and logical Memory IDs, and a fresh Ed25519 public key for the next policy version. More than 100 eligible Sessions fails closed; there is no hidden remainder treated as shared. Team stores the immutable manifest, hash, key, and the eligible subset fixed at preparation. The Desktop broker compares the Team echo with a freshly read owner-local manifest before requesting Native approval and again before consuming it. Destination occupancy can only remove an approved Session from issuance. It cannot make an unapproved Session eligible.

Only the owner-local edge holds the private key, sealed under the owner envelope with owner, Personal Project, backend, Team Project, device, link epoch, and policy-version associated data. The link epoch changes when a Project is unlinked or relinked, so returning to the same Team Project does not restore an old policy. Future capture proof is prepared before the first source write while that exact Project and policy are active. A new ordinary managed Conversation can qualify without a Project folder; Project HQ cannot. The signed payload binds the Personal owner and distinct Team principal, Team member, device, policy, Session, logical Memory, source and mapping revisions, capture context, candidate and closure hashes, expiry, and nonce. A delayed association, an old capture, a moved-in Session, a private Session, or a changed policy version has no eligible stamp. Team stores only the approved public key and atomically consumes the proof nonce with one issue.

Before owner-local staging, a durable local issue reserves the exact Session, Project, backend, link epoch, policy version, mapping, and source revision. Team then reserves a one-use issue in the same transaction that creates an existing Pending Share candidate and outbox work. The grant remains unavailable until sanitized source closure and current authority pass. Publication locks Team, membership, Workspace, access, Project, member link, source root, and admission hold, then checks the issue, active policy version, source identity, consent ceiling, and private exclusion before making the existing grant available. This issuance cannot replace a richer source-wide owner policy or disturb a grant in another Workspace. After activation, the grant advances under its own consent and freeze rules, including separately approved Curated Step-up. Team issue status reconciles a published grant into owner-local `project_policy_session_grants` provenance so the existing HQ107 freeze-before-detach flow can review it. A staged issue remains a move barrier until it is settled.

Enable and revoke use exact Native Action Grants. The owner-local attempt journal is written before dispatch and binds the Project, Team principal, device, Action Grant, request, version, manifest, and payload hash. A lost response is recovered from the exact encrypted Team execution receipt; a general policy status or an absent receipt cannot prove success. Unknown attempts stay visible and block another policy effect. Local enable/revoke is finalized only from that receipt; the old mutation is never replayed after restart.

Make private first records an owner-local pending privacy intent, then writes a durable Team exclusion that fences unactivated policy issues and explicit offers. If a grant is active, the owner must complete its exact Native grant revocation and receive the withdrawal acknowledgment before the Personal Conversation exclusion is committed. Cancellation or uncertain acknowledgment remains pending. Conversation and Project deletion inventories include reserved and staged local issues and explicit offers, so their existing source admission holds cover the backend before any grant is active. Team publication checks that hold under the source-root lock. Move-out obtains an exact Team exclusion and owner-local move fence even for a Conversation with no grant, then uses HQ107 freeze-before-detach for any activated grant. A known canceled move can release its exact fence; an unknown outcome stays held. A moved-in existing Conversation and Share again require a fresh Native Project-bound Pending Share, whose exact Project binding is inserted with publication. Share again creates a new grant identity while the revoked old grant remains revoked. Its explicit consent does not lift the stable Conversation exclusion for later captures. These paths cannot acquire policy eligibility from current placement alone.
